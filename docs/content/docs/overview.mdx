---
title: Overview
description: What shrinkit does.
---

shrinkit compresses macOS screen recordings and speeds them up. It takes a `.mov`, `.mp4` or
`.m4v` file and encodes it with ffmpeg into an `.mp4`, by default at twice the speed and without
sound. It can also cut stretches out of a recording and join several recordings into one.

Three ways to use it:

- [Right-click in Finder](/docs/right-click/): pick a [preset](/docs/presets/)'s entry, and the result lands beside the recording.
- [Watch folder](/docs/watch-folder/): drop a recording into `input/`, and pick the result up from `output/`.
- [Command line](/docs/command-line/): `shrinkit recording.mov`.
